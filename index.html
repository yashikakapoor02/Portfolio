My co-op experience is helping me see that testing is more than checking whether a feature works. It involves understanding expected behaviour, reviewing test cases, documenting defects clearly and communicating with developers. As I continue in the role, I want to become more confident in explaining test results and understanding the reasoning behind test coverage.

Engineering projects have shown me the importance of breaking a problem into smaller steps, testing assumptions and using evidence to find the source of an issue. I want to apply this approach more consistently, including techniques such as the 5 Whys.

Working on projects and learning in a team environment has reinforced that technical knowledge is only part of engineering. Explaining an issue clearly, listening to other perspectives and sharing progress are also important parts of completing work effectively.
